FAQ

Questions students commonly ask.

Do I need an existing business idea to join?

No. You can start with an idea or learn to identify market opportunities during the program.

Will I build a complete business proposal?

Yes. Projects cover market research, Business Model Canvas, MVP design, GTM strategies, and pitch decks.

Is this program only for founders?

No. The skills are equally valuable for business development, startup operations, growth, and product roles.

Will I receive an Internship Certificate and LOR?

Yes, upon fulfilling program requirements, you will receive an Internship Completion Certificate and Recommendation Letter.

What topics are covered in financial modeling?

You will learn unit economics, pricing models, CAC, LTV, margin analysis, and basic revenue projections.

Is employment or funding guaranteed?

No. The program offers structured skill development and practical internship experience; employment, funding, or stipends are not guaranteed.
