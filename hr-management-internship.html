Frequently Asked Questions

GET ANSWERS TO COMMON QUESTIONS.
Is this program suitable for HR beginners?
+
Yes, the curriculum begins with core HR fundamentals before advancing into practical recruitment and operations.
Will I work on recruitment and sourcing?
+
Yes, candidate sourcing, job descriptions, screening rubrics, and interview coordination are core topics.
Will I receive a certificate and LOR?
+
Yes, after fulfilling program requirements, you will receive an Internship Completion Certificate and Recommendation Letter.
What tools will I learn?
+
You will learn recruitment workflows, ATS concepts, HR analytics in Excel/Google Sheets, and documentation templates.
Is HR Analytics included?
+
Yes, you will learn key workforce metrics like time-to-hire, attrition rate, cost-per-hire, and data visualization.
Is employment or stipend guaranteed?
+
No. The program offers structured skill development and practical internship experience; employment or stipends are not guaranteed.
