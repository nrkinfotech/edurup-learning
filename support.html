Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question to view its detailed answer.

How do I receive my Internship Certificate and LOR? ⌄
Upon successfully completing your 6-month internship program and submitting your required projects, you will receive an official verifiable Internship Completion Certificate and a Letter of Recommendation (LOR) issued by Edurup Learning.
What is the registration fee for the 6-month internship program? ⌄
The complete 6-month career-focused internship program has a single registration fee of ₹9,999, which covers all 10+ real-world projects, live classes, mentor support, certificate, and LOR.
Are the projects real-world industry projects? ⌄
Yes! All 10+ projects included in each internship domain (Data Analytics, Data Science, AI, Full Stack, Digital Marketing, HR, Sales, Entrepreneurship) are designed around actual industry use-cases with dedicated mentor guidance.
Can I balance this internship with my college classes? ⌄
Absolutely! The internship program features flexible schedules and recorded session access so students can smoothly manage their college coursework alongside internship learning.
How are my doubts resolved during the internship? ⌄
You get dedicated mentor support through live Q&A sessions, WhatsApp student channels, and 1-on-1 mentor guidance to resolve any technical or project doubts promptly.
